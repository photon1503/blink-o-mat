## 3. Baseline Extraction Notes

It builds a coarse aspect-correct grid with a 96-cell short side, interpolates measured star FWHM using a Gaussian kernel, applies robust ramp bounds, and stretches the resulting bitmap uniformly. Statistics use true star min/max/mean plus corner-versus-center interpolated averages. The live tooltip samples the grid under the pointer.
